# Detailed Lesson Sequence

The recommended full competition pathway contains **75 scheduled sessions**:

```text
67-session core pathway
+ 8-session competition sprint
= 75 scheduled sessions
```

Ordinary lessons use the seven-step 75-minute after-school club flow. Long mocks and reproductions use their stated competition-realistic duration.

This is a compressed scheduled pathway. The larger `02_Class_Missions` curriculum bank contains additional lessons for reteaching, deeper practice, alternative resources, and extension.
