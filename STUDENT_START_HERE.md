1. Skill Warm-Up

5. Guided Practice

Watching a video, finishing a chapter, or running an example once is not completion. You must explain, reconstruct, test, modify, analyse errors, and record evidence.

## Sessions 33–40 — Reading Evidence

Read the assigned section of Melanie Mitchell’s *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans* before class. Bring:

- one central claim;
- one supporting case;
- one unresolved question;
- one AI claim that should be tested rather than accepted immediately.

A chapter summary is not enough. Separate the author’s argument, the evidence, and your own judgement.

## Sessions 41–43 — Mathematics Transition

Before formal model study, you will learn to move between:

```text
task meaning
↔ symbols and shapes
↔ equation
↔ graph
↔ hand calculation
↔ code
↔ model behaviour
```

You must be able to identify `X`, `y`, parameters, predictions, loss, metric, and array shapes; calculate a small prediction/loss; explain one gradient-descent update; and distinguish probability, threshold, class, loss, and metric.

## Model-Recognition Habit

For every task, identify `X`, `y`, label availability, required output, task family, baseline, metric, candidate models, mathematical objects, and likely limitations before choosing an algorithm.
