# Student Start Here

This repository tells you **what to learn, what to practise, and what evidence to submit**. Your graded work belongs in your own GitHub repository.

Your teacher will assign a pathway and specific missions. You are **not** expected to complete every lesson file in the curriculum bank.

## What You Do Every Ordinary Class

Each ordinary 75-minute after-school club lesson follows the same cycle:

1. **Skill Warm-Up** — use the exact assigned video, guide, document, or task segment.
2. **Talk Robin 1** — explain what you learned and what is still confusing.
3. **Entry Check** — prove that you understand the prerequisite idea.
4. **Core Pattern** — identify the reusable method, decision rule, or reasoning pattern.
5. **Guided Practice** — practise with teacher support.
6. **Independent Rebuild** — recreate or transfer the pattern without copying the example.
7. **Talk Robin 2 + Evidence** — explain, submit, and record proof of learning.

The goal is not just to finish the task. The goal is to **rebuild and explain it independently**.

## What Mastery Means

- **Level 1 — Recognise:** identify terms, structures, task types, metrics, and code patterns.
- **Level 2 — Explain:** explain the idea and model decision in your own words.
- **Level 3 — Calculate / Trace:** calculate metrics and trace code, data flow, or tensor shapes.
- **Level 4 — Rebuild / Debug:** rebuild without copying and repair errors.
- **Level 5 — Modify / Apply:** adapt the method to a new dataset or task.
- **Level 6 — Compete:** solve an unfamiliar problem under time, compute, validation, and submission constraints.
