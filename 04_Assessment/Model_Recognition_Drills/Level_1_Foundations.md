# Level 1 — Foundation Scenarios

Complete the full model-recognition answer format for each scenario.

### Day 1 — Apartment price
A dataset contains floor area, district, building age, floor, lift availability, renovation condition, and the final sale price of previously sold apartments. Predict the sale price of a newly listed apartment.

### Day 2 — Spam filtering
Past emails are labelled spam or not spam. The system must produce a probability and a final spam decision for each incoming email. Missing a dangerous phishing email is more costly than moving one legitimate newsletter to spam.

### Day 3 — Customer groups
A retailer has purchase frequency, average basket value, product-category proportions, and days since last purchase for each customer. No customer-group labels exist. The business wants a small number of interpretable customer segments.

### Day 4 — Sensor failure
A factory has millions of normal vibration windows but only a handful of confirmed failures. The immediate goal is to flag unusual new windows for engineer review, not to name the exact failure type.

### Day 5 — Student score
For past students, study hours, attendance, assignment completion, prior score, and final examination score are available. Predict the final score for current students.

### Day 6 — Disease screening
Patient records include symptoms, age, exposure history, and a laboratory-confirmed positive or negative diagnosis. The screening tool should minimise missed positive cases while still reporting a calibrated probability.

### Day 7 — Image categories
Each training image has one label from cat, dog, rabbit, or bird. The model must assign exactly one class to each new image.

### Day 8 — Product similarity
Each product has a numeric embedding created from its description and image. Given one product, return a ranked list of the most similar products. There is no single class label.

### Day 9 — Reduce dimensions
A dataset has 300 correlated numeric measurements per sample. The team wants a two-dimensional representation for visual inspection while preserving as much major variation as possible.

### Day 10 — Churn
Historical subscribers are labelled as renewed or cancelled. The company needs a ranked outreach list and can contact only 5% of active subscribers.

### Day 11 — Energy forecast
Hourly electricity use, temperature, weekday, holiday, and lagged consumption are available. Predict electricity use for each hour tomorrow.

### Day 12 — Game agent
An agent observes a game state, chooses an action, and receives delayed rewards. No correct action label is supplied for each state. The goal is to learn a policy that maximises long-term reward.
