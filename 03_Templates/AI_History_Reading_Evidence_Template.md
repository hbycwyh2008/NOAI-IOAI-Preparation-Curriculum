## Before Class

### 1. Author’s Central Claim

Write one sentence that states the author’s main claim in the assigned reading.

### 2. Evidence From the Text

Identify one concrete historical event, experiment, system, result, or failure used to support the claim.

### 3. Claim Boundary

What does the evidence support? What larger conclusion does it not establish?

### 4. Unresolved Question

Write one question that cannot be answered by a plot summary.

### 5. AI Claim to Audit

Write one claim from the reading, media, a product description, or class discussion that should be tested rather than accepted immediately.

## After Class

### Revised Position

What did you believe before class, and what changed after examining the evidence?

### Remaining Uncertainty

What new evidence would strengthen, weaken, or change your conclusion?

### AI-Assistance Record

Record any AI assistance used. State what was generated, what was independently checked, and what was rewritten or rejected.

## Submission Gate

The evidence is complete only when it contains:

- a clear claim;
- specific textual evidence;
- a bounded conclusion;
- an independent reconstruction;
- a revision or uncertainty statement;
- an AI-assistance record when applicable.
