# Universal Evidence Checklist

Use this checklist at the end of every ordinary lesson.

## Required Evidence

- [ ] I completed the Skill Warm-Up notes or task.
- [ ] I answered the Entry Check.
- [ ] I wrote the Core Pattern in my own words.
- [ ] I completed the Guided Practice.
- [ ] I completed the Independent Rebuild.
- [ ] I explained my rebuild to a partner or in writing.
- [ ] I recorded one mistake, misconception, or revision.
- [ ] I added an AI-use note if I used an AI assistant.

## Evidence Quality Levels

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 — Present | Evidence exists but is incomplete or copied. |
| 2 — Understandable | Evidence shows basic understanding. |
| 3 — Rebuildable | Evidence shows the student can recreate the method with less help. |
| 4 — Transferable | Evidence shows the student can apply the pattern to a new case. |
| 5 — Competition-ready | Evidence is accurate, concise, timed, and defensible. |
