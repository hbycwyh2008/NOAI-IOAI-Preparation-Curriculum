# 22 — Audio and Speech

This module prepares students for NOAI/IOAI audio tasks such as waveform inspection, spectrogram/Mel feature extraction, source-aware classification, and ASR/TTS workflow awareness.

## Lessons

- [Lesson 01 — Waveforms, sample rates, spectrograms, and Mel features](lesson-01.md)
- [Lesson 02 — Audio classification plus ASR/TTS workflow](lesson-02.md)
- [Lesson 03 — Mel-spectrogram to image-classification bridge](lesson-03-mel-spectrogram-image-bridge.md)
- [Lesson 04 — Audio dataset leakage, augmentation, and evaluation](lesson-04-audio-leakage-augmentation-evaluation.md)
- [Lesson 05 — Round 2 audio reproduction and submission strategy](lesson-05-round2-audio-reproduction.md)

## Minimum Evidence

Students should submit an audio feature explanation, spectrogram/Mel diagram, leakage warning, baseline comparison, and Round 2-style audio submission plan.
