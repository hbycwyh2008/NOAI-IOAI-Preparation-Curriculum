**Duration:** 75 minutes

## Required Resource
Andrew Ng MLS Course 1: Logistic Loss and Regularized Classification.

## Learning Targets
- explain why squared error is unsuitable here;
- connect probability, threshold, and predicted class;
- train and evaluate `LogisticRegression`.

## Guided Practice
Vary the threshold and record changes in false positives and false negatives.

## Independent Task
Rebuild the classifier and justify the chosen threshold for a new scenario.
