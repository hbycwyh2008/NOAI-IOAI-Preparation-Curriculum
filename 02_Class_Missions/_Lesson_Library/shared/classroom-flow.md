# Our Classroom Flow

Every class follows this clear learning cycle.

## Core Cycle

**WE LEARN. PRACTICE. REBUILD. SHARE.**

| Step | Block | Time | Student output |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | **Skill Warm-Up** | 0–8 min | Use a video, guide, or task to meet the idea. |
| 2 | **Talk Robin 1** | 8–15 min | Pair discussion: what was done and what is confusing. |
| 3 | **Entry Check** | 15–22 min | Check basic understanding before we go deeper. |
| 4 | **Core Pattern** | 22–35 min | Teacher explains the key method or pattern clearly. |
| 5 | **Guided Practice** | 35–53 min | Practice with teacher support. Ask questions and improve. |
| 6 | **Independent Rebuild** | 53–67 min | Recreate the pattern on your own with less help. |
| 7 | **Talk Robin 2 + Evidence** | 67–75 min | Summarize, explain, and submit proof of learning. |

## Goal

The goal is not just to finish the task. The goal is to **rebuild and explain it independently**.

## Non-Negotiable Rule

Do not replace this cycle with a lecture-first routine. Every normal after-school club session must preserve all seven blocks in this order. Long mock contests and extended project simulations may use special contest timing, but the ordinary lesson cycle remains the seven-step flow above.
