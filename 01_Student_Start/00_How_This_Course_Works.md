# How This Course Works

This is not a video-watching course. Every ordinary 75-minute after-school club lesson follows the same seven-step learning cycle:

1. **Skill Warm-Up** — use a video, guide, or task to meet the idea.
2. **Talk Robin 1** — discuss what was done and what is confusing.
3. **Entry Check** — show basic understanding before going deeper.
4. **Core Pattern** — learn the key method or pattern clearly.
5. **Guided Practice** — practise with teacher support, ask questions, and improve.
6. **Independent Rebuild** — close the example and recreate the pattern with less help.
7. **Talk Robin 2 + Evidence** — summarize, explain, and submit proof of learning.

**WE LEARN. PRACTICE. REBUILD. SHARE.**

The goal is not just to finish the task. The goal is to **rebuild and explain it independently**.

Your teacher may ask you to explain any submitted code, calculation, trace, or written answer orally.
