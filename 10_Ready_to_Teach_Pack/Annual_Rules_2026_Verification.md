# Annual Rules Verification — 2026

**Last public-source review:** 2026-08-04  
**Event status at review:** IOAI 2026 is in progress; final post-event task/environment validation is not yet available.  
**Scope:** dated public IOAI/NOAI facts and operational assumptions that may change. Organiser-supplied handbooks, participant instructions, and later clarifications remain authoritative.

## Confirmed Current IOAI Information

- IOAI 2026 is scheduled for **2–8 August 2026 in Astana, Republic of Kazakhstan**.
- The public schedule identifies the event week and daily programme in Astana.
- The scientific competition design describes an educational **At-Home Round** with three tasks, followed by **Contest 1**, which continues the at-home task families, and **Contest 2**, which introduces novel tasks.
- The public 2026 contest-rules page covers the Individual Contest, Team Challenge, GAITE Contest, and recognition system.
- Official rules state that omissions, inconsistencies, and technical details may be updated before the event.
- IOAI’s separate AI Models Track is not part of the official human contestant ranking and must not be confused with the student competition pathway.

## Confirmed Current NOAI China Information

- The public NOAI academic-support page identifies a **2026 NOAI syllabus V1.5** and separates the first-round level test from the second-round application-practice stage.
- The public description emphasises Python programming, machine-learning foundations, PyTorch application, and large-language-model knowledge.
- The public NOAI FAQ states that the China competition environment is offline by default; contestants cannot generally import arbitrary downloaded pretrained models.
- When a task requires a specific pretrained model, the organiser may provide it locally and document its use in the baseline notebook.
- Permitted libraries, models, and assets remain subject to the current annual organiser instructions; this repository must not infer permission from a previous year or from general library availability.

## Curriculum Rule

Dated official documents and organiser instructions override this repository for:

- competition duration, schedule, stage, and submission limits;
- internet, website, and external-data access;
- AI-assistant and external-API permissions;
- permitted pretrained models and organiser-provided local assets;
- Python and package versions;
- GPU, CPU, RAM, storage, and platform limits;
- individual, team, GAITE, selection, and eligibility formats;
- scoring, ranking, medal, appeal, and clarification procedures;
- file naming, output schema, checkpoints, and submission validation.

No lesson may tell students that a tool is allowed merely because it was allowed in an earlier year. Historical environment and LLM rules are examples, not guarantees.
